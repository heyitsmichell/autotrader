# Options strategy catalog (research, 2026-11-25)

Backtest numbers: `data/experiments/options_research/REPORT.md` (Black-Scholes model, 2011-01 to 2025-11-21; ~800 prior registry trials, so treat any best result as deflated). Webull levels from [Webull help](https://www.webull.com/help/faq/10980-Available-Options-Strategies): L1 covered calls, buy-writes, cash-secured puts; L2 adds long calls/puts, collars, protective puts, long straddles/strangles (margin); L3 spreads/condors; L4 naked options (min $10k). Cash accounts reach L2 at most. App support: single-leg BUY/SELL_TO_OPEN/CLOSE only; multi-leg orders exist in the broker layer but are not wired into execution.

| Strategy | Rules | Published evidence | Failure periods | Level / capital | App can run now? | Our test |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| Put-write (CBOE PUT) | Sell 1-month ATM S&P put each month, fully cash collateral | PUT 9.5% vs S&P 9.8% CAGR, stdev 9.95% vs 14.93%, Sharpe 0.65 vs 0.49, 1986-2018; max DD 32.7% vs 50.9% ([Bondarenko 2019](https://cdn.cboe.com/resources/education/research_publications/PutWriteCBOE19_v14_by_Prof_Oleg_Bondarenko_as_of_June_14.pdf), [Cboe summary](https://www.cboe.com/insights/posts/white-paper-shows-volatility-risk-premium-facilitated-higher-risk-adjusted-returns-for-put-index/)) | 2008, Mar 2020, 2018 | L1, full strike in cash | Yes (single leg SELL_TO_OPEN put; needs expiry/assignment handling) | Real ^PUT 2011-25: 7.8%, Sharpe 0.53, DD -30% vs SPY 13.7%/0.83. Model SPY 30-delta: 7.7%/0.78 |
| Buy-write / covered call (BXM, BXMD) | Own index, sell 1-month ATM (BXM) or 30-delta (BXMD) call | BXM 9.1% vs PUT 10.4% annualised mid-1986 to early 2012 ([ACG for Cboe](https://cdn.cboe.com/resources/indices/documents/pap-assetconsultinggroup-cboe-feb2012.pdf)); PUT beat BXM ~1.1%/yr, put-call parity ([AQR](https://www.aqr.com/-/media/AQR/Documents/Insights/White-Papers/AQR-PutWrite-vs-BuyWritevF.pdf)); returns come mostly from equity beta, short vol Sharpe ~1 but <10% of risk ([Israelov and Nielsen](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2444999)) | Sharp V-rebounds (2009, Apr 2020): call caps upside, keeps downside | L1, own 100 shares | Needs shares held; single-leg SELL_TO_OPEN call is supported | Model SPY 16.2%/1.29 (optimistic: flat VIX, no skew); do not rely |
| Collar | Own stock, buy put, sell call | Underperforms index; CLL beta 0.62, loses ~1.3%/yr to the VRP when net long vol ([Israelov and Klein](https://papers.ssrn.com/sol3/papers.cfm?abstract_id=2704518), [CXO](https://www.cxoadvisory.com/equity-options/equity-index-collar-performance/)) | Strong bull years | L2 | Two legs: not wired | Not tested |
| Volatility risk premium | Implied > realised on average; systematic short vol harvests it | Underlies PUT/BXM Sharpe edge (Bondarenko above) | Feb 2018, Mar 2020 | L1 to L4 | Via put-write | VRP-positive filter lowered return (5.9% vs 7.7%) |
| IV vs RV stock option selection | Sort stocks by RV minus ATM IV; buy straddles where RV high, sell where IV rich | Long-short straddle 22.5%/month gross, 7.5% after costs ([Goyal and Saretto, JFE 2009](http://www.ruf.rice.edu/~jgsfss/goyal_041808.pdf)) | Costs, illiquid options | L2 long / L4 short straddle | Needs real chains, straddles: no | Not testable (no single-stock IV history) |
| Earnings straddle | Buy ATM straddle ~3 days before earnings | +3.34% average pre-announcement, 1996-2013 ([Gao, Xing, Zhang JFQA 2018](https://ideas.repec.org/a/cup/jfinqa/v53y2018i06p2587-2617_00.html)); concentrated in small, illiquid names | Large-cap liquid names, costs | L2 | Straddle order not wired; earnings calendar exists in app | Not tested (no IV history) |
| Momentum/signal via options | Long calls on trend signals | Prior app test: calls did not beat stock on Sharpe (`data/experiments/options_entry/REPORT.md`) | Chop, theta | L2 | Yes (BUY_TO_OPEN) | Signals as CSP: 4.7%/0.83 vs stock 7.9%/1.00 |
| 0DTE / short-dated selling | Sell same-day index spreads | Mostly defined-risk, balanced flow; no robust selling-return evidence ([Cboe](https://www.cboe.com/insights/posts/volatility-insights-evaluating-the-market-impact-of-spx-0-dte-options/)) | Gap/trend days | L3 | No (spreads, intraday) | Not tested |
| Protective put / tail hedge | Buy 5-10% OTM put monthly | PPUT 6.6% vs PUT 9.5% CAGR since 1986 (Bondarenko above): hedge is expensive | Grinding declines (2022) | L2 | Yes, single leg BUY_TO_OPEN | 5% OTM costs ~2 pts CAGR, 10% OTM 0.7 pts in model |
| Wheel | CSP until assigned, then covered calls above basis | No peer-reviewed evidence found; combines put-write and buy-write | Persistent downtrends (stuck below basis) | L1 | Possible but needs assignment tracking | Model SPY 12.7%/0.93 (model-dependent) |
